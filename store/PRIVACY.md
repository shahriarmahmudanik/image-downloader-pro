# Privacy Policy – Image Downloader Pro

_Last updated: 2026-11-15_

Image Downloader Pro does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

- **What it reads:** When you click the extension icon, it reads the image URLs on the current tab to show them to you. This happens locally in your browser.
- **What it sends:** Nothing. The extension has no servers, analytics or tracking, and makes no network requests of its own. Images you choose to download are fetched by Chrome directly from the website hosting them.
- **What it stores:** Nothing. Downloaded images are saved to your Downloads folder.
- **Support link:** The popup contains an optional "Buy me a coffee" link to my Ko-fi page (ko-fi.com/shahriaranik). It opens in a new tab only if you click it. The extension itself sends no data to Ko-fi; any information you give there is handled under Ko-fi's own privacy policy.
- **Optional features (ZIP, format conversion, resizing, remove background):** To save images as a ZIP, convert or resize them, or remove their background, the extension needs to read the image files themselves. The first time you use one of these, Chrome asks for permission to access websites (an optional permission; plain downloads do not need it). The images are processed on your computer only. Background removal uses an AI model that is bundled inside the extension and runs locally; no image, URL or other data is uploaded anywhere.
- **Permissions:** `activeTab` and `scripting` to scan the current page; `downloads` to save the images you select. Optional: access to websites, requested only when you use ZIP, convert, resize or remove background.
