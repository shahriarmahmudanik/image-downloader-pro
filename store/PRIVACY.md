# Privacy Policy – Image Downloader Pro

_Last updated: 2026-10-22_

Image Downloader Pro does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

- **What it reads:** When you click the extension icon, it reads the image URLs on the current tab to show them to you. This happens locally in your browser.
- **What it sends:** Nothing. The extension has no servers, analytics or tracking, and makes no network requests of its own. Images you choose to download are fetched by Chrome directly from the website hosting them.
- **What it stores:** Nothing. Downloaded images are saved to your Downloads folder.
- **Permissions:** `activeTab` and `scripting` to scan the current page; `downloads` to save the images you select.
